The PABRA bean programme in Tanzania tested 26 bean entries with 715 farmers in four tricot trials that started in 2024. Since 2021 the programme has run 25 trials with 2,866 farmers in 55 districts, from Kagera in the north-west to Mbeya and Songwe in the south-west. The trials follow the tricot approach (de Sousa et al. 2024). CIAT produced the data with TARI, and all of it is open in tricot-data.

Farmers rated test plots better than their own variety more often each year. In 2024 they did so for 74% of 360 plots on 120 farms, up from 54% of 633 plots in 2021. This is the programme's clearest signal that the entries it brings to farmers now outperform what farmers already grow.

Farmers did not single out one entry in 2024. The one trial with a final overall ranking, TrctPO3, did not separate its 12 entries (34 farmers, p = 0.71). The three trials planted later in the year have rankings up to the reproductive stage only, and none of them separated entries on plant survival.

Across seasons no entry dominated. Of the 16 entries ranked for overall preference in three or more trials, TARIBEAN 1 came closest, in the top three in 4 of 10 trials. Three of the 11 trials with an overall ranking separated their entries at the 5% level.

The 2024 season renewed the pipeline. 25 of the 26 entries tested in 2024 were new to the programme's tricot trials, with CALIMA UYOLE as the only entry kept as a comparison since 2021.

## Section 3: Results of the 2024 season

**Medium seed, first season.** In TrctPO3, ECAPAN 021, listed as a historical check in the metadata, had the highest log-worth (1.09, standard error 0.55, ranked on 9 farms). The release candidate ADP-640 followed (0.60, 0.56, 7 farms). With 34 farmers for 12 entries, each entry was ranked on six to nine farms, too few to separate them. Treat both as a group of top entries to test again.

**Large seed, two seasons.** Sugar 106 ranked high on plant survival in both large-seed trials, first in AVISAPO3 (31 farmers) and second in AVISAPO3B2 (133 farmers). Red mottled 102 led AVISAPO3B2. Plant survival at flowering is an early signal and the final ranking will decide.

**Comparison with the local variety.** Farmers judged the test plots better than their own variety on 60% to 89% of plots, highest in the two trials of the second season. Yield was recorded on 96 farms of the first three trials and is not analysed here, because its units are flagged as unclear in part of the data.

## Section 7: Key result story

### Three in four test plots now beat farmers' own beans

Farmers in Tanzania rated 74% of 360 test plots better than their own variety in 2024, up from 54% of 633 plots in 2021.

Each season TARI and CIAT give every farmer a package of three bean entries. At harvest the farmer says, plot by plot, whether the entry did better or worse than the bean they usually grow. Since 2021, 838 farms gave 2,514 such answers in 20 trials of the PABRA network.

The share of plots rated better rose every year, from 54% in 2021 to 70% in 2022, 72% in 2023 and 74% in 2024. The intervals of 2021 and 2024 do not overlap, so the rise is larger than the play of chance among farms.

The comparison mirrors the choice a farmer makes when deciding which seed to plant next season. The two trials of the second 2024 season scored highest, with 85% and 89% of plots rated better, on 225 registered farms each.

The comparison is with each farmer's own variety, so the entries changed between years and so did the local varieties and the districts. The result shows that the entries the programme puts in front of farmers now compare better with what they grow. It does not show which single entry drives it, and the final rankings of the second 2024 season will tell.

## Limitations and next steps

- Three of the four trials of 2024 hold rankings up to the reproductive stage only. The report gives plant survival for them and leaves overall preference to the final data.
- Few farmers completed the final overall ranking. Since 2021, 11 trials gave enough overall rankings to fit a model, 2 more gave fewer than 15, and 12 gave none.
- The comparison with the local variety pools different entries, districts and local varieties across years. It shows a trend in what farmers saw, not the effect of one variety.
- Yield was recorded in part of the trials, with units flagged as unclear for some sites. It is not used here.
- Entries are counted by the name recorded in the data. Some names may refer to the same line, which the programme should confirm.
- The programme has not given its outcomes, budget or training records, so those fields are empty. The Impact Areas, SDGs and readiness level are suggestions from fixed rules and wait for the programme to confirm them.
- This is a test draft computed in Python where R was not available. It is not reviewed by the authors.
