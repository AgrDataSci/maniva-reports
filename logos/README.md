# Logos

Logos the team can place on a report. Each is cut from a publication the team supplied, so the marks are the ones CGIAR and its centres used in 2025 and 2026. They are not redrawn, recoloured or stretched.

| File | Mark | Use on | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cgiar.svg`, `cgiar.png` | CGIAR with the tagline "Science for a food-secure future" | White pages | CGIAR Portfolio Narrative 2025, cover |
| `cgiar-no-tagline.svg`, `.png` | CGIAR without the tagline | White pages, small sizes | Same |
| `cgiar-white.png` | CGIAR, white | Dark covers and bands | Name tag of the Opportunity Crops India Workshop, 2026 |
| `alliance-bioversity-ciat.svg`, `.png` | Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT | White pages | CGIAR Portfolio Narrative 2025, back cover |
| `alliance-bioversity-ciat-white.png` | Same, white | Dark covers and bands | Name tag, 2026 |
| `crop-trust-white.png` | Crop Trust, white | Dark covers and bands | Name tag, 2026 |
| `africarice`, `cifor`, `cimmyt`, `cip`, `icarda`, `icrisat`, `ifpri`, `iita`, `ilri`, `irri`, `iwmi`, `world-agroforestry`, `worldfish` (`.svg`, `.png`) | CGIAR centres | White pages | CGIAR Portfolio Narrative 2025, back cover |

SVG files are vector and go in the PDF. PNG files are 8 times the printed size and go in the Word version. White variants exist only as PNG, with a transparent background.

## How to use them

List the files under `logos` in `report.yml`, with paths relative to the report folder, for example `../../logos/cgiar.svg`. The renderer places them at equal height in one row on the cover. Use the white variants only on a dark background.

## Rules

- Use the logo of an organisation only on a report that organisation took part in, and only with the rights its people grant. CGIAR and each centre publish brand guidelines, and those govern.
- Never redraw, recolour, crop or stretch a logo. Add a missing logo only from a file the organisation or the authors supply.
- The Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT is changing its name at the end of November 2026. Replace its logo here once the new mark is out, and keep the legal name, Bioversity International or CIAT, in the text of a report.
- These marks belong to their organisations. They are not covered by the licence of this repository.
